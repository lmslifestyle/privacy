Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026
Provided by: The Stars Education
App: LMS_YiJing · Android package: com.lmsyijing.app

LMS_YiJing is a Yi Jing study and divination app using the Mei Hua Yi Shu method. This policy describes the app's handling of information and the optional explanation features.

No registration, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Casts are calculated on your device. If you use the optional question features, your question and cast context are sent directly to DeepSeek to generate an explanation.

1. On-device features and information we do not request

You can cast hexagrams and read the study material without an account or entering personal information. The app does not request your name, email address, phone number, contacts, GPS location, or advertising identifier. It does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

The app does not deliberately attach an account identifier, device identifier, contacts, or GPS location to explanation requests. However, text you choose to type may itself contain personal information. Please do not enter names, contact details, financial information, health information, or other sensitive information about yourself or others.

2. Information held on your device

The app saves your Chinese or English language preference locally. Current casts, questions, and clarification conversations are held in app memory; the app does not maintain a saved history database. Clearing the cast or making a new cast removes its displayed reading and conversation. This does not delete information already transmitted to DeepSeek.

3. Optional DeepSeek explanations

The app uses the DeepSeek API, a third-party language-model service, for two features:

  • Cast summary: if you enter a question before casting, the app requests an explanation automatically when it displays that cast.
  • Clarification: sending a follow-up question requests an answer about the current cast.

These HTTPS requests can include:

  • The question you entered, including the original question about the cast.
  • The current clarification conversation, including previous questions and generated answers, when you send a follow-up.
  • Cast context: generated numbers, derivation steps, primary/mutual/changed hexagrams, moving line, trigrams, five-element relationship, and relevant reference interpretations.
  • Your requested answer language and instructions for generating the explanation.

The purpose is to provide the explanation you requested. The request goes from your device directly to DeepSeek; the app developer does not operate an intermediary server or database for these conversations. Like other services receiving direct internet requests, DeepSeek can also receive technical connection information such as your IP address.

DeepSeek handles submitted information under its own applicable terms and privacy policy. Consult that policy for its processing locations, uses, retention, and privacy rights. We do not promise that DeepSeek processes requests only in your country or deletes them immediately after generating an answer.

If the summary request fails, the app can show a short explanation generated on your device. A failed clarification request shows an unavailable-service message.

4. Retention, deletion, and your choices

We do not maintain a developer-run server-side archive of your casts or conversations. This does not mean that the third-party provider retains no information; provider retention and deletion are governed by its own policies and applicable terms.

  • Leave the question box empty and do not send clarification questions to use casting and learning without DeepSeek explanation requests.
  • Clear the current cast to remove the displayed session. Already transmitted requests cannot be recalled by clearing the app.
  • Remove the app's local data through your device settings or uninstall it to remove its locally stored preference, subject to your operating system's backup and restore behavior.
  • For privacy questions or requests, contact us at the email below. There is no app account to delete. We can address information under our control and help direct provider-related requests, but cannot guarantee deletion from DeepSeek's systems.

5. Security

Explanation requests use HTTPS to encrypt data in transit. No transmission or storage system is completely secure. Avoid putting personal or sensitive information in your questions.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information through the app.

7. This policy website

This static page is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub may process technical information when serving the page. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when this policy changes.

Privacy contact: The Stars Education — lilianex2017@gmail.com.

隐私政策

LMS_YiJing 是一款采用梅花易数方法的易经学习与占卜应用。本政策说明应用及可选解读功能如何处理信息。

无需注册，没有广告、数据分析或追踪 SDK。起卦在您的设备上计算。如果使用可选提问功能，您的问题及卦象背景会直接发送给 DeepSeek，以生成解读。

一、设备本地功能及我们不主动索取的信息

您无需注册账号或提供个人信息，即可起卦和阅读学习资料。应用不主动索取您的姓名、电子邮件、电话号码、通讯录、GPS 位置或广告标识符，也不包含广告、数据分析或崩溃报告 SDK。

应用不会主动在解读请求中附加账号标识、设备标识、通讯录或 GPS 位置。但您自行输入的文字可能包含个人信息。请勿输入您本人或他人的姓名、联系方式、财务资料、健康资料或其他敏感信息。

二、设备上保存的信息

应用在本地保存您的中文或英文语言偏好。当前卦象、问题和追问对话保存在应用内存中；应用不建立已保存的历史记录数据库。清除卦象或重新起卦会移除该次显示的解读和对话，但不会删除已发送给 DeepSeek 的信息。

三、可选的 DeepSeek 解读

应用通过第三方语言模型服务 DeepSeek 的 API 提供两项功能：

  • 卦象小结：如果起卦前输入了问题，显示该卦时应用会自动请求生成解读。
  • 进一步了解：发送追问时，应用会请求生成针对当前卦象的回答。

通过 HTTPS 发送的请求可能包括：

  • 您输入的问题，包括该次起卦的原始问题。
  • 发送追问时的当前对话，包括之前的问题及生成的回答。
  • 卦象背景：随机数字、推导步骤、本卦、互卦、变卦、动爻、上下卦、五行生克关系及相关参考释义。
  • 您选择的回答语言及生成解读的指令。

发送上述信息是为了提供您请求的解读。请求从您的设备直接发送给 DeepSeek；应用开发者不为这些对话运营中转服务器或数据库。与其他接收直接互联网请求的服务一样，DeepSeek 还可能接收 IP 地址等技术连接信息。

DeepSeek 按照其自身适用条款及隐私政策处理提交的信息。关于处理地点、用途、保存期限及隐私权利，请查阅其政策。我们不承诺 DeepSeek 仅在您所在国家处理请求，也不承诺其在生成回答后立即删除信息。

如果卦象小结请求失败，应用可显示设备本地生成的简短解读；追问请求失败时会显示服务暂时不可用的提示。

我们不运营用于归档您卦象或对话的开发者服务器。但这并不代表第三方服务不保存任何信息；第三方的保存及删除方式受其自身政策和适用条款约束。

  • 不填写起卦问题，也不发送追问，即可在不发出 DeepSeek 解读请求的情况下使用起卦及学习功能。
  • 清除当前卦象可移除显示中的会话；清除应用内容无法撤回已发送的请求。
  • 通过设备设置清除应用本地数据或卸载应用，可移除本地语言偏好；具体情况受操作系统的备份及恢复机制影响。
  • 如有隐私问题或请求，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。应用没有需要注销的账号。我们可以处理控制范围内的信息，并协助指引第三方相关请求，但无法保证删除 DeepSeek 系统中的信息。
