English

Effective date: 1 November 2026

The Stars Education provides LMS_YiJing for Yi Jing study and reflection. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Information and local storage

We do not ask you to register or provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location. Casting and study features run on your device. Your language preference is saved locally. Current casts and conversations are held in app memory, without a saved history database.

Optional question features

If you enter a question before casting, the app sends it to an external language-model service to generate a summary. Sending a follow-up also sends the current conversation. Requests include the cast details and your selected language, and use HTTPS.

We do not operate a server that stores these conversations. The external service may process submitted text and technical connection information, such as your IP address, under its own terms. We cannot guarantee that it retains no data. Please do not enter personal or sensitive information.

Your choices and deletion

You can use casting and study features without sending questions. Clearing a cast or making a new one removes its displayed reading and conversation, but does not delete information already sent to the external service. Clear local app data or uninstall the app to remove the stored preference, subject to your device’s backup settings. Contact us for privacy or deletion requests concerning information under our control.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will appear on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact The Stars Education at lilianex2017@gmail.com.

LMS_YiJing 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

The Stars Education 提供 LMS_YiJing，供易经学习与思考之用。应用没有账号、广告、数据分析或追踪 SDK。

信息与本地存储

我们不要求注册，也不主动索取您的姓名、电子邮件、电话号码、通讯录或位置。起卦与学习功能在您的设备上运行。语言偏好保存在本地；当前卦象和对话保存在应用内存中，不建立已保存的历史记录数据库。

可选提问功能

如果起卦前输入了问题，应用会将其发送给外部语言模型服务，以生成卦象小结。发送追问时，还会发送当前对话。请求包括卦象资料和您选择的语言，并通过 HTTPS 传输。

我们不运营用于存储这些对话的服务器。外部服务可能按其自身条款处理提交的文字及 IP 地址等技术连接信息，我们无法保证其不保存任何数据。请勿输入个人或敏感信息。

您的选择与删除

您可以不发送问题，仅使用起卦与学习功能。清除卦象或重新起卦会移除显示中的解读和对话，但不会删除已发送给外部服务的信息。清除应用本地数据或卸载应用可移除语言偏好，具体受设备备份设置影响。如有涉及我们控制范围内信息的隐私或删除请求，请联系我们。

儿童

本应用面向年满 18 岁的成年人。我们不会有意向儿童索取个人信息。

政策变更

本政策可能更新，变更内容及新的生效日期将在此页面公布。
