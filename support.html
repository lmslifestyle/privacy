Learn Mei Hua Yi Shu

LMS_YiJing combines learning topics, reference material for all 64 hexagrams, and practice readings. It is for cultural study and personal reflection.

How do I start learning?

Open the Learn tab to explore the foundational topics and worked casting example. Browse the hexagrams and expand an entry to read its explanation.

How do I make a practice reading?

Open the Cast tab and use the Shake button or shake your phone. A question is optional. Expand the Auspicious Number card to examine the numbers and derivation.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings and select Chinese or English. Your preference is saved on your device.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. Learning material and casting calculations are available on your device. Optional question-based explanations require internet access and send your question, conversation context and cast details to an external service. Please avoid personal or sensitive information. Generated explanations can be inaccurate.

What if an explanation fails to load?

Check your connection and try again later. You can continue using the learning material and casting calculations. If the problem persists, contact technical support with the app version, device model, iOS or Android version, and steps to reproduce it. Remove personal information from any screenshots.

学习梅花易数

LMS_YiJing 结合基础教学、六十四卦参考资料及练习卦象，供文化学习与个人思考之用。

如何开始学习？

打开「学习」，阅读基础主题及起卦实例。浏览六十四卦，展开条目查看释义。

如何生成练习卦象？

打开「摇卦」，点击摇卦按钮或摇动手机。问题为选填项。展开「吉数」卡片，可查看数字及推导过程。

如何切换语言？

打开「设置」，选择中文或英文。语言偏好保存在设备本地。

需要账号或网络吗？

无需账号。学习资料及起卦计算在设备上运行。可选的问题解读需要网络，会将问题、对话背景及卦象资料发送给外部服务。请勿输入个人或敏感信息。自动生成的解读可能不准确。

无法加载解读时怎么办？

请检查网络连接，稍后再试。您仍可使用学习资料与起卦计算。如问题持续，请向技术支持提供应用版本、设备型号、系统版本及重现步骤。发送截图前请移除个人信息。
